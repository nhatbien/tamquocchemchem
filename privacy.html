Bie Studio ("we") respects your privacy. This policy covers Chop Chop Kingdoms: 1 vs 100 (Tam Quốc Chém Chém) on iOS and Android.

Data we collect

The current version does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Game progress (gold, items, cleared stages, settings) is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game collects no data from any user, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising or other third-party services, we will update this policy before that version is released and state exactly what data is collected.
